# WCS — avatar image prompts (10 fighters)

How to use: paste **BLOCK A** first, then one **character block** after it. One
character per generation. Keep BLOCK A byte-identical across all ten so the set
reads as one product line — that consistency is the whole point.

If your tool supports a style/character reference image, feed it the existing
Wally studio render as the style anchor and it will hold the line even harder.

---

## BLOCK A — house style (paste before every character)

```
Studio product photograph of a collectible designer art toy, shot on a seamless
pure-white cyclorama. Matte vinyl/PVC finish with a soft fine-grain texture — no
gloss, no clearcoat, no metal except where the character calls for it. Smooth
rounded forms, generous fillets, chunky simplified anatomy, zero sharp corners.

Full body, standing, centred, feet flat, arms relaxed at the sides unless noted.
Three-quarter front view, camera at chest height, slight upward tilt so the
figure reads as a monument. 85mm lens look, mild perspective, no fisheye.

Lighting: large soft key from the upper front-left, cool fill from the right, a
subtle rim to separate the figure from the background, and a soft contact shadow
directly under the feet. Even, product-catalogue exposure — bright but never
blown out, shadows open and readable.

Flat graphic colour blocking, high-contrast graphic accents, no gradients across
large surfaces. Clean vector-crisp edges where two colours meet.

Negative: no text, no logos, no watermarks, no brand marks, no real-world
trademarks, no busy background, no props beyond those described, no extra
characters, no drop shadow behind the figure, no motion blur, no grain overlay.
```

## Character blocks

### 1. WALLY — The Tokenization Titan
```
Subject: a stylised grey elephant. Large egg-shaped head, tall and slightly
narrow, widest above the eyeline. Enormous wide floppy ears standing out well
past the head on both sides. Long trunk hanging down the centre of the chest
with a soft curl at the tip and faint horizontal ring creases. Two short blunt
cream-white tusks either side of the trunk root. Full rounded pear-shaped body,
widest at a generous belly. Thick soft arms hanging down close to the flanks,
ending in rounded mitten hands with four short stubby finger bumps. Thick column
legs, simple rounded feet. Small tufted tail.
Colour: uniform warm light grey body, off-white cream tusks.
Signature: oversized matte-black wraparound sunglasses with a flat straight brow
bar, sitting high on the face, and one bold white lightning-zigzag glint mark
painted on each lens — identical on both lenses, not mirrored, leaning up to the
right. The sunglasses are the only black and the glint is the only white.
Attitude: unbothered, deadpan, quietly enormous.
```

### 10. THE BLACKISH BULL — Lord of the Final Pump (final boss)

Signature: no clothing, no props. It is a statue. Optional faint green glow in
the eyes.
Attitude: implacable, monumental, final.

## Notes

- Generate one at a time; batching ten into one image collapses the style.
- For a reference SHEET like Wally's, append to BLOCK A:
  `Four views of the same figure on one sheet: front, side, back, three-quarter.`
- If a character comes out too detailed/realistic, add: `simplified, toy-like,
  fewer details, larger forms.` The failure mode is always too much detail.
- Keep the palette lines — they are what makes the ten read as one roster.
